# Modern Mustard Seed

We build websites, AI voice agents, agentic systems and custom software for small businesses, managed for you, end to end. The business keeps what we build: the code, the accounts and the know-how stay in its name.

Modern Mustard Seed is an AI studio in Kalispell, Montana, serving businesses across the United States. Sarah Scarano builds. Anthony Scarano runs sales and marketing.

## MCP servers we run

Sixteen remote MCP servers. Fifteen are published in the [official MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io/v0/servers?search=com.modernmustardseed), and nine are listed in Anthropic's Claude directory, marked below.

| Server | What it does | Docs |
|---|---|---|
| Site Check | A measured health check for any public website, with a fix for every problem. Listed in Anthropic's Claude directory. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/site-check) |
| AI Search Kit | What AI answer engines read: crawler rules, structured data, llms.txt and name, address and phone consistency. Listed in Anthropic's Claude directory. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/ai-search-kit) |
| Domain Health | Whether email from a domain will land, and whether the domain is safe and owned well. Listed in Anthropic's Claude directory. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/domain-health) |
| Own It | Who holds the keys to a business online, and how hard each would be to leave. Listed in Anthropic's Claude directory. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/own-it) |
| Call Desk | Whether a call is legal right now, with calling hours, recording consent and AI disclosure rules cited. Listed in Anthropic's Claude directory. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/call-desk) |
| Voice Agent Linter | Checks a voice agent's prompt and config against rules learned on live calls. Listed in Anthropic's Claude directory. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/voice-linter) |
| Dry Run | Simulates a year of cash for a small services business thousands of times. Listed in Anthropic's Claude directory. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/dry-run) |
| Idea to Product Sequencer | Tests the riskiest assumption in a product idea first and sequences the build. Listed in Anthropic's Claude directory. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/idea-sequencer) |
| Local Trades Kit | Service areas, seasonal demand and review timing for contractors and home services. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/trades-kit) |
| Package Pricing Lab | Turns costs, capacity and deliverables into fixed packages with the margin math shown. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/pricing-lab) |
| Small Business Funding Finder | Live federal grant listings from Grants.gov, in plain words. | [docs](https://mcp.modernmustardseed.com/docs/funding-finder) |
| Chief Dreamer | A private, lasting memory for your ideas inside Claude, behind your own sign-in. | [dreamer.modernmustardseed.com](https://dreamer.modernmustardseed.com) |
| Book It | Puts a home-service business inside Claude and ChatGPT so people can send it a booking request. | [book.modernmustardseed.com](https://book.modernmustardseed.com) |
| Holiday Calendar | U.S. federal holidays and business-day math, the worked example in our starter kit. | [starter.modernmustardseed.com](https://starter.modernmustardseed.com) |
| Cross + Covenant Bible | Scripture reading and a daily devotional from Cross + Covenant. | [docs](https://crossandcovenant.co/bible-connector) |
| Cross + Covenant Church Year | The Sunday lectionary and the seasons of the church year. Listed in Anthropic's Claude directory. | [docs](https://crossandcovenant.co/church-year-connector) |

## Open source

- [local-growth-crew](https://github.com/ModernMustardSeed/local-growth-crew): a Claude plugin with four agents, seven skills and five of the servers above, for auditing and growing a local business online.
- [small-business-rules](https://github.com/ModernMustardSeed/small-business-rules): open, sourced rule data for calling hours, call recording consent, AI voice disclosure and voice agent lint rules.
- [small-business-mcp-starter](https://github.com/ModernMustardSeed/small-business-mcp-starter): the Next.js template we use to ship a remote MCP connector, with evals, rate limits and policy pages.
